ChargeSmart Grid Platform Overview

AI-driven EV Charging Load Forecasting, Dynamic Pricing & Grid Stress Mitigation for Indian Discoms

EV Driver App

Find cheap, grid-safe stations. Real-time pricing nudges to incentivize off-peak charging.

  • Live Map with Green / Yellow / Red station stress
  • "Navigate & Save ₹40" smart route recommendation
  • Time-of-Day 30% off discount nudge
  • 24-hour dynamic tariff curve per station
Operator Portal

Manage charging hubs, track feeder health, and approve AI-suggested dynamic pricing.

  • Real-time revenue & utilization graphs
  • Feeder overload predictive alerts (Hours to RED)
  • Dynamic price suggestions with 1-click approval
  • Off-peak coupon generator & upgrade requests
Discom Admin

City-wide grid intelligence: simulate EV adoption growth and site new charging stations.

  • Heatmap of 50 distribution transformers
  • EV Adoption Growth Simulation Lab (+50% load)
  • Siting Tool: Click anywhere on map → Top 3 sites
  • Time-of-Day policy cascade & stress reports
